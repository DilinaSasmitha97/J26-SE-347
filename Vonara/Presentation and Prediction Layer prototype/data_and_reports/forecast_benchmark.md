# Forecasting benchmark (rolling origin)

Generated by `evaluate_forecasting.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## Protocol

- Series: 66 SYNTHETIC, 100 M4_DEV, 100 M4_TEST, 1000 M4_TEST_LARGE
- M4_DEV: 100 monthly series sampled uniformly at random (seed 2026) from the 48,000 monthly series of the M4 competition dataset (100,000 series in total), each truncated to its last 36 observations; development data
- M4_TEST: 100 further monthly series (seed 2027) drawn from the 47,900 M4 monthly series not in M4_DEV, same truncation; held out in Stage 2 and evaluated then; re-run here with the Stage 8 code, so it is no longer unseen
- M4_TEST_LARGE: 1,000 further monthly series (seed 2028) drawn from the 47,800 M4 monthly series in neither earlier sample, same truncation; drawn after the Stage 8 design was frozen and evaluated once
- 4 origins per series, 3 periods apart, horizon 3; every forecast uses only data before its origin
- MASE is scaled by the in-sample one-step naive MAE of each training window
- SARIMA order: (0, 1, 1)x(0, 1, 1)_12
- Deployed settings (forecast_config.json 2026-10-07.1): guard_sarima True, fitted_smoothing_min_points 48, band_method normal, clip_non_negative True, noise_limit 0.2, conflict_noise_limit 0.08
- THETA and AUTO_ETS are statsmodels comparison baselines with their own 95% intervals, clipped at zero like the deployed layer; they fall back to naive when a fit fails
- An UNRELIABLE window has sMAPE > 20%
- Environment: python 3.13.14, numpy 2.5.1, scipy 1.18.0, statsmodels 0.14.6, platform Windows-11-10.0.26200-SP0
